AmanahCare — Care Remittance Platform
Joy University Ideathon 2026 • MVP Concept | Team AmanahCare

Simulate how a migrant worker orders intent-locked wellness care for their aging parents via WhatsApp, with escrow payment protection and verified health outcomes.

WhatsApp Order & Escrow Lifecycle STATUS: COMPLETED & VERIFIED

Solving "The Triple-Anxiety Loop"

Millions of migrant workers support aging parents from thousands of kilometers away. Sending cash does not ensure care. AmanahCare replaces unrestricted cash with guaranteed care outcomes.

💊
Pharma-Direct
Prescription and chronic medicines delivered reliably to parents' doorsteps, eliminating the struggle to reach pharmacies.
🩺
Elder-Check Visits
Scheduled home visits by vetted local nurses who check vital statistics (BP, pulse, blood glucose) and transmit live updates to the sender.
🚨
24/7 SOS Alert Response
1-tap emergency button & voice assistance triggering immediate local emergency assistance and alerting family members abroad.

4-Tier Technology Architecture

1. User Layer: WhatsApp Business API + Multilingual AI Chat for zero app-download friction.
2. Orchestration: Node.js / Python API backend engine handling order routing and caregiver assignment.
3. Trust & Escrow Layer: Provider verification & smart payment ledger. Funds held in escrow until verified photo & vital proof.
4. Service Layer: Asset-light network of vetted local pharmacies, clinics, and community nurses.
